KEY BATTLES OF THE CIVIL WAR — CONTENT NOTES
===========================================

This revision keeps the same general historical grounding as the original package but expands middle-school context in three areas requested by the user:
1. landing-page background information before the Confederate-flag section
2. political context (emancipation, changes in generals, Lincoln’s 1864 reelection, etc.)
3. fun “Did You Know?” battle facts

The Confederate-flag visuals are now supplied as temporary image assets rather than inline drawings in the page source.

SEPTEMBER 7, 2026 — OVERVIEW / FLAG-HISTORY CHECKS
---------------------------------------------------
National Park Service — Civil War Overview
https://www.nps.gov/civilwar/overview.htm
Traditional count: 620,000 military deaths; NPS notes recent scholarship places the number at 750,000 or more.

National Park Service — Southern Battle Flags (Pea Ridge NMP)
https://www.nps.gov/peri/learn/historyculture/southern-battle-flags.htm
Explains battlefield confusion with the Stars and Bars, the lack of one universal Confederate Army battle flag, and postwar popularity of rectangular versions because they were easier for manufacturers to mass-produce.

Smithsonian National Museum of American History — The Flag in the Sixties
https://amhistory.si.edu/starspangledbanner/the-flag-in-the-sixties.aspx/default.aspx
Documents use of Confederate flags by white segregationists opposing federal intervention and civil rights during the 1960s.

Smithsonian National Museum of American History — Confederate Battle Flag
https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_439645
Documents the square Army of Northern Virginia battle-flag form and its development after First Bull Run.
