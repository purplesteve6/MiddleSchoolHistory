NOTE ON TERMINOLOGY
-------------------
The Atlanta/Sherman page explains that “total war” is common school-textbook language, while “hard war” is often a more precise term for Union strategy in 1864–65. The Wilderness/Spotsylvania page separately defines attrition rather than treating every 1864 operation as the same strategy.
