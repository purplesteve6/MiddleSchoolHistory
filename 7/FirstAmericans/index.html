The First Americans

Indigenous peoples of the Americas and New York before and during early European contact.

Unit Overview

North America was home to many different Indigenous societies long before Europeans arrived. Geography, climate, natural resources, migration, trade, and cultural traditions shaped how communities developed in different regions.

  • Theories about the first human settlement of the Americas
  • Regional Native cultures and the influence of geography
  • Mississippian mound-building societies and the city of Cahokia
  • Haudenosaunee life, government, and culture in what is now New York
  • Early encounters between Indigenous peoples and Europeans
