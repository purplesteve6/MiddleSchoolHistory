EDITORIAL GUARDRAILS
Culture regions are approximate study groupings, not tribal borders.
Mississippian is not one tribe or empire. Descendant communities belong in the account.
Analyze the bias of “Five Civilized Tribes”; never use it as a neutral hierarchy.
Cherokee writing is a syllabary. Newspaper claim: first Native American newspaper in the United States.
Forced removals used multiple land and water routes; Cherokee main removal 1838–1839.
Seminole history includes earlier Florida peoples and incoming towns. Florida was not empty.
Three Seminole Wars; distinguish Florida survivors and families removed west.
Patchwork developed especially in the early 1900s. Traditions can change.
Private ceremonial knowledge and images are not required for this educational overview.
