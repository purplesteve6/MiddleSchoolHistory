CONTENT NOTES
-------------
- “Northwest Coast,” “Pacific Northwest Coast,” and “Pacific Northwest” overlap but are not identical. These pages use “Pacific Northwest Coast” for the classroom branch and “Northwest Coast” for the standard culture-region label. The separate Plateau branch remains planned and is not folded into this coastal unit.
- “Coast Salish” is explicitly presented as a broad language/culture grouping containing many separate sovereign nations, not as one tribe.
- “Chinookan” is used for the broad language/culture family; “Chinook Indian Nation” is used for the modern Nation representing five western Chinookan tribes.
- The regional page deliberately replaces the old “nomadic” quiz language with a permanent-village + seasonal-resource-camp model.
- Monumental poles and formline art are not presented as generic pan-Native symbols. Clan ownership, artist/nation context, and purpose should be included whenever images are added.
- Public traditional stories are summarized only when a Native organization has already made the teaching public and ownership/context can be stated. Restricted ceremonial details are not reproduced.
