NATIVE AMERICAN FONT INSTALLATION
=================================

The site files in this update are wired to self-host the two supplied fonts so
visitors see the intended typography even when the fonts are not installed on
their devices.

Create this folder in the repo if it does not already exist:
  /assets/fonts/native-american/

Place the two ORIGINAL font files you supplied into it with these exact names:
  TribalSchoolhouse-v2XM.ttf
  AztecWay-OVRr6.otf

Expected final paths:
  /assets/fonts/native-american/TribalSchoolhouse-v2XM.ttf
  /assets/fonts/native-american/AztecWay-OVRr6.otf

The shared stylesheet is:
  /7/FirstAmericans/native-american-fonts.css

Once those two files are in the repo and pushed to GitHub, the fonts are served
by the site itself rather than relying on a student's computer.
